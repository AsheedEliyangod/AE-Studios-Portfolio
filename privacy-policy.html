AE Studio develops LOST LIGHT 1. You can play the adventure offline without creating an account. This policy covers the Google Play closed test for Android, version 1.0 (version code 1). It explains what stays on your phone and how AE Studio handles game stability data.

Your saved game

The game stores your checkpoint, position, inventory, equipment, currency, story progress and claimed rewards in app storage on your device. It keeps a local backup for recovery. These records let Continue restore your adventure and prevent the same reward from being granted twice. AE Studio does not operate a cloud save service for the game.

Ads and purchases

Rewarded ads and real-money purchases are disabled in this closed test. The game does not start Google’s ad-consent flow, request ads or open a real-money checkout. Items bought with gold or diamonds earned in the adventure use only in-game currency.

Game stability

Unity Diagnostics collects crash logs, technical diagnostics, other app performance data and an installation-specific identifier. Unity processes these data for AE Studio so we can identify crashes and understand how the game performs. Diagnostics are enabled for this build and there is no in-game switch to turn them off.

Diagnostic data are encrypted in transit. Unity documents a default retention period of 90 days for personal data in Diagnostics. See Unity’s Diagnostics privacy overview.

Privacy requests

To request access to or deletion of your diagnostics, email AE Studio using the contact details below. Unity uses an installation ID to locate an individual installation’s records. This version has no player-facing display for that ID. If we cannot identify your installation, we will explain the limitation when we respond.

Younger players

LOST LIGHT 1 is intended for ages 13–15, 16–17 and adults aged 18 and over. It is not designed for children under 13. Local rules about children’s data and the age at which a teenager can consent vary. If you are a teenager and need help understanding a privacy choice, ask a parent or guardian. Parents and guardians may contact AE Studio about their child’s information using the email below.

Retention, deletion and security

Local progress stays in the app’s storage across play sessions. To erase it from the device, open Android Settings, select LOST LIGHT 1 under Apps, then choose Storage and clear the app’s data. This also removes the local backup and can permanently erase your progress.

Android’s app storage protections help protect local records. Clearing the app’s data erases the local save and backup. It does not delete diagnostic records already sent to Unity.

Your rights and how to reach us

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal data, object to or restrict its use, withdraw consent, or complain to a data protection authority. Email asheedeliyangod@gmail.com for questions or requests concerning AE Studio. Include “LOST LIGHT 1 privacy” and describe your request. Please leave out passwords and save files.

If you email us, we receive your address and message and use them to address your request. We may need enough information to verify the request.

We will update this page and its effective date when our practices change.
