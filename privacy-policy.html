AE Studio develops LOST LIGHT 1. You can play the adventure offline without creating an account. This policy explains what stays on your phone and when the game’s advertising and consent services may contact Google.

Your saved game

The game stores your checkpoint, position, inventory, equipment, currency, story progress and claimed rewards in app storage on your device. It keeps a local backup for recovery. These records let Continue restore your adventure and prevent the same reward from being granted twice. AE Studio does not operate a cloud save service for the game.

Optional rewarded ads

The shop shows what you can earn before you choose to watch. Completing an eligible ad can grant 50 in-game gold or one health potion. The game records a reward claim locally after Google’s reward callback. If ads are unavailable, you can continue playing.

The Android game includes Google AdMob and Google’s User Messaging Platform. The consent service may contact Google when the app starts. Where consent settings permit, the ads SDK can initialize and request an ad when you open the rewards shop, before you decide whether to watch. The current configuration requests Google test ads; production ad serving is disabled.

When these services connect, data sent to Google can include:

  • Your IP address, which Google may use to estimate your general location. The game does not request precise GPS location for ads.
  • Device or account identifiers, such as the Android advertising ID when available and the app set ID.
  • App and ad interactions, such as launches, taps and video views, and technical diagnostics such as app launch time, crashes or hangs.

Google uses these data to deliver and measure ads, understand performance and prevent fraud. Your saved campaign is separate from these ad requests. Read how Google uses information from partner apps and Google’s Android ads SDK disclosure.

Consent and privacy choices

The game checks Google’s consent status on Android. Where Google requires a consent form, the game requests that form. When Google requires an ongoing privacy entry point, open Settings → Privacy choices to revisit your selection. Available choices depend on your region and Google’s configuration. You can also manage the advertising ID in your Android device’s privacy settings.

Younger players

LOST LIGHT 1 is intended for ages 13–15, 16–17 and adults aged 18 and over. It is not designed for children under 13. Local rules about children’s data and the age at which a teenager can consent vary. If you are a teenager and need help understanding a privacy choice, ask a parent or guardian. Parents and guardians may contact AE Studio about their child’s information using the email below.

Retention, deletion and security

Local progress stays in the app’s storage across play sessions. To erase it from the device, open Android Settings, select LOST LIGHT 1 under Apps, then choose Storage and clear the app’s data. This also removes the local backup and can permanently erase your progress.

Android’s app storage protections help protect local records. Google’s Mobile Ads SDK encrypts data in transit using TLS. Google manages retention and deletion for information its services receive, separately from your phone’s save files. See Google’s retention policy.

Your rights and how to reach us

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal data, object to or restrict its use, withdraw consent, or complain to a data protection authority. Email asheedeliyangod@gmail.com for questions or requests concerning AE Studio. Include “LOST LIGHT 1 privacy” and describe your request. Please leave out passwords and save files.

If you email us, we receive your address and message and use them to address your request. We may need enough information to verify the request. For data Google controls, see Google’s privacy policy and controls.

We will update this page and its effective date when our practices change.
